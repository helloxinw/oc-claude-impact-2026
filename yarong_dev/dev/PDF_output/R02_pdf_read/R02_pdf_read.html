Packet R02 · 22 pages · adult, 73

73-year-old woman (adult criteria) referred from the emergency department; she was not admitted (face sheet: Patient Class Emergency, inpatient admit N/A). EMS brought her from a grocery store parking lot. She reports being homeless for about 5 days, has no emergency contact, and has Medicare plus CalOptima Medi-Cal. Referral reason: right leg cellulitis, shin to toes. She was on oral doxycycline for 8 days and has felt 'sicker' for 2 days; in the ED she got IV cefepime, vancomycin and an LR bolus. Afebrile (36.6 C), HR 72, RR 16, SpO2 98% on room air, BP 178/79 on losartan. WBC 7.46, lactate 0.8, procalcitonin 0.10 (flagged), flu/RSV/COVID NAAT negative (date redacted), CXR without acute findings. PMH: paroxysmal AF s/p ablation, asthma, OSA on CPAP, bipolar 1, schizophrenia, GERD/Barrett, anemia, back injury. AOx4. Forms report independent ADLs, 100 ft with a seated walker and self-administered meds. Missing or open: blood/urine culture results, right foot X-ray and LE venous duplex (pending), MRSA swab, troponin, EF, a confirmed ECG read, 5 sets of vitals, PT/OT, psych and surgical notes marked as attached, and a discharge/antibiotic/home-health plan (MDM, impression and disposition are unfilled templates). Contradictions: apixaban status (med list BID vs 'taken off it recently' vs form 'No'), walking ability, and IV antibiotics (form 'No' vs given in ED). No psychiatric medications are listed.
