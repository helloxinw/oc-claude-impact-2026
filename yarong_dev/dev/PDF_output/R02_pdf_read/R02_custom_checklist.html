Request to the referrer

Plain-language list for the hospital case manager, required items first.

  1. Send the blood culture (x2) and urine culture results.
  2. Send the PT/OT evaluation (marked as attached on the referral form but not included), showing how far she walks with her walker and how much help she needs.
  3. Send the last 5 sets of vital signs with times (temperature, heart rate, blood pressure, SpO2), including readings that show her blood pressure is stable on oral losartan over 24 hours.
  4. Send the finished ED provider note with the diagnosis, decision-making and discharge plan filled in.
  5. Send the discharge medication list and the oral antibiotic she will go home on (name, dose, how long), and confirm she has a 30-day supply of all medications.
  6. Send the confirmed (final) EKG reading.
  7. Tell us whether she is currently taking Eliquis (apixaban). The medication list, the doctor's note and the referral forms disagree. If she is, confirm she can manage it herself.
  8. Send the psych notes marked as attached, and tell us whether she is on any psychiatric medication or treatment for bipolar disorder and schizophrenia.
  9. Confirm she has been screened for suicidal thoughts and for command hallucinations, with the results.
  10. Tell us whether she is, or has ever been, under a conservatorship or a Full Service Partnership (FSP).
  11. Tell us whether she is on any isolation precautions.
  12. Fill out the current IHH referral form (RC0725), including the front-page screening checklist.
  13. Tell us whether IV antibiotics or an IV line will continue after the ED. If so, send the home health order to maintain the IV and give the medication.
  14. Confirm she is going straight from the ED (not being admitted). If she is admitted, send a negative MRSA swab and a negative COVID swab taken within 24 hours of arrival.
  15. Send the right foot X-ray and leg venous duplex results when they are final.
  16. If available: her ejection fraction (from the prior TEE or an echo) and whether it is her baseline; any respiratory therapy notes; whether she has her CPAP machine and uses any breathing treatments beyond the PRN inhaler.
