Request to the referrer

Plain-language list for the hospital case manager, required items first.

  1. Send the History & Physical.
  2. Send a negative MRSA swab result.
  3. Send a negative COVID swab collected within 24 hours of the planned transfer.
  4. Send a psychiatry note or physician note confirming the patient is psychiatrically stable on current medications, including a suicide-risk screen.
  5. Send a current orientation (AOx) exam and clarify whether the dementia diagnosis is current.
  6. Tell us whether the patient is under, or being considered for, a conservatorship or Full Service Partnership.
  7. Confirm whether the patient can self-administer all medications, including as-needed lorazepam and temazepam.
  8. Send a current discharge medication list with doses.
  9. Send recent progress notes (the ones included are fragments).
  10. Send the second-step TB skin test result.
  11. Send an OT evaluation and a cosigned copy of the PT note.
  12. Confirm whether the patient is in an acute hospital or a skilled nursing facility.
  13. Complete the current IHH referral form (RC0725).
  14. If available: an EKG, and any notes describing the patient's 'sudden mood changes'.
